How to Play Pixel Disc

Pixel Disc is a browser-based, pixel-art disc golf game. Your goal is to reach the basket in as few throws as possible, navigating trees, water, roads, rocks and other obstacles along the way.

1. Choose where to play

Select Daily Challenge for the daily round or choose a named course from the course selector. You can also open Explore Courses to browse holes, or Game Modes to practice without starting a normal round.

2. Aim and prepare your throw

At the tee, use the on-screen controls to choose your throw direction, power and release. Consider the distance to the basket and the hazards in your line. A high-power throw may travel farther, but accuracy and control matter around obstacles.

3. Watch the flight

After throwing, watch your disc follow its flight path. Trees and other solid obstacles can deflect the disc, while out-of-bounds areas can affect your score. Adjust your next throw based on where the disc lands.

4. Finish the hole

Continue throwing until your disc reaches the basket. Your score is measured against par: birdie means one throw under par, par means matching the target, and bogey means one over. The objective is to complete the round with the lowest total score.

5. Try different ways to play

Open Field is a place to experiment with disc flight and distance. Ace Run focuses on hole-in-one attempts. Practice individual holes to learn the layout before returning to a full round.

6. Customize and track your play

On the homepage, tap the disc in the logo to change its color. Check the leaderboards to compare scores where available, and use My Profile to see your player information. Settings contains options for your preferred controls.

Tips for improving

  • Prioritize a clear landing area instead of always using maximum power.
  • Account for trees, water and out-of-bounds boundaries before committing to a shot.
  • Use practice modes to learn how power and release affect distance and direction.
  • On tighter holes, a shorter controlled throw can be better than a risky long one.
