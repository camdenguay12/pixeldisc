About Pixel Disc

Pixel Disc is a small, independent disc golf game designed for quick rounds in your browser. It combines approachable pixel-art visuals with the decisions that make disc golf fun: choosing a line, controlling a throw and working around the landscape.

What you can do here

Play a Daily Challenge, select from multiple courses, explore individual holes and practice your throwing technique. Different environments introduce their own obstacles and scenery, including trees, rocks, ponds and out-of-bounds areas. Leaderboards and player features provide ways to follow your results.

Designed for short sessions

The game is built with mobile play in mind, but can also be enjoyed on a desktop browser. You can start a round, return to it later when a saved round is available, or use a practice mode when you only have a few minutes.

Why pixel art?

Pixel art keeps the courses readable and gives each environment a distinctive style. The visuals are intentionally simple so that the important elements—your disc, the basket, the fairway and hazards—remain easy to recognize.

Learning the game

New to Pixel Disc? The How to Play guide explains the controls, scoring, hazards and practice modes. If you're new to disc golf itself, start by focusing on accuracy and choosing safe landing spots rather than maximum distance.

Advertising and the player experience

Pixel Disc may display advertising to help support ongoing development. Ads are separate from the game and its navigation. The goal is to keep gameplay and informational content usable without requiring visitors to interact with advertisements.

Feedback

Pixel Disc continues to evolve with new gameplay refinements, course improvements and interface changes. If you notice an issue, please share it through the project's public GitHub issue tracker.
